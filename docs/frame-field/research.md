## Photography practices and editorial references

A desktop Chrome pass captured actual rendered pages and inspected navigation. A second 390 px pass inspected the menus and named destinations of seven references. Follow-up visits included the Mumbai and Pune service pages, a filtered portfolio, a booking form, and Kander’s series index. The observations below describe visible patterns and how they inform this concept.

| Practice | Observed pattern | Frame & Field decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bharat Sikka](https://www.bharatsikka.com/) | A tight index of named bodies of work sits above an oversized series title. Each title feels like an editorial proposition. | Give each project a name, then place its assignment category and creative objective nearby. |
| [Nadav Kander](https://www.nadavkander.com/works-in-series) | The series index pairs evocative project titles with precise photograph counts. The header separates commissions, series and editorial material. | Distinguish the curated edit from the full archive. Give gallery viewers a frame count and a way back to their project. |
| [Tim Walker](https://www.timwalkerphotography.com/) | Selected work, portraiture, stories, moving image and books have distinct places in the index. Individual editorial entries include identifying captions. | Caption the project beneath its cover. Let a portrait and a narrative series support different reading rhythms. |
| [Studio Kleiner](https://www.carlkleiner.com/) | A single dramatically lit object occupies a black field beneath a densely classified index of subjects and media. | Use a dark surround for fullscreen photography. Keep a short list of labelled filters outside the frame. |
| [Bela Borsodi](https://www.belaborsodi.com/) | A red sculptural form, a fashion portrait and a glass still life sit immediately adjacent with very little interface between them. | Edit the adjacency of dissimilar subjects on the homepage. Add captions wherever a visitor must choose a route. |
| [Adam Gibson](https://www.adamgibson.com.au/) | Small and large architectural photographs share the opening with an overscale masthead. The project list is a second entry point. | Compose the first viewport around a deliberate focal image. Move project discovery into a clearly labelled index. |
| [Olivia Rae James](https://www.oliviaraejames.com/) | A wedding diptych places a vertical colour photograph beside a horizontal monochrome frame. Generous paper margins keep the subjects separate. | Let event and couple galleries alternate scene-setting and close detail. Preserve the original shapes in the viewer. |
| [Peter McKinnon](https://www.petermckinnon.com/) | An edge-to-edge contact sheet efficiently presents a large body of work. Plain subject navigation gives visitors an alternative to scanning every image. | Make a dense archive available after the introductory edit. Keep search and subject filters visible together. |
| [Platon](https://www.platonphoto.com/) | A distinctive high-contrast identity frames a very short set of routes to photography, film, biography and books. | Use a recognisable wordmark with limited type treatments. Leave stronger colour to the images. |
| [Rankin](https://www.rankin.co.uk/) | A black release takeover with a white action and art-directed cover makes a publication feel important. It also requires dismissal before browsing. | Give a single featured shoot an immersive dark spread in the natural reading order. Keep the entrance immediate. |
| [Stories by Joseph Radhik](https://storiesbyjosephradhik.com/) | A close gesture carries the opening wedding photograph. Scope and voice appear beside it. The welcome screen adds a step to the work. | Open a wedding case with a human detail and provide a direct gallery link. |
| [Two Mann Studios](https://twomann.com/) | Its opening action leads straight to work, and the photographers introduce themselves as people further down. | Position work first. A short photographer introduction can explain what a nervous sitter can expect. |
| [Art + Commerce](https://www.artandcommerce.com/) | Artist names, editorial projects, an image archive, production and films have distinct labels. Credits belong alongside specific work. | Put production notes inside a case study. Keep the commercial quote workflow accessible from the studio navigation. |
| [Girish Joshi, Pune](https://www.girishjoshiphotos.com/product-photographer-in-pune/) | The product service identifies packaging, e-commerce and marketing outputs, with an explanation of working method. The homepage also exposes editorial, headshots, interiors and food. | Explain each service in terms of who uses the files, the formats required and preparation before the shoot. |
| [7-Frames Studios, Pune](https://7-frames.in/) | Browsing and quotation have separate actions. The phone menu includes a named booking destination and its enquiry collects a short subject and brief. | Support visitors with a defined session and visitors who need a custom production estimate. Preserve the service selection across that transition. |
| [AD Design Studio, Mumbai](https://aakrutidedhia.in/contact) | Its form asks for the shoot type, tentative date and location. The service cards link to filtered portfolio URLs. One category showed an empty state on inspection. | Deep-link archive filters. Test category URLs directly and give visitors a way to clear an empty result. Ask about shoot details before contact details. |
| [MK Studio, Mumbai](https://www.marketincrewstudios.com/services/food-photography) | Its food service names delivery-app, menu and social outputs. The site publishes starting prices and a separate booking destination. | Show the estimated price beside explicit inclusions. Distinguish an enquiry about availability from a confirmed date. |
| [Aman Kurien Studio, Mumbai](https://www.amankurienstudio.com/about) | An uncluttered biographical page connects specialisation in food and hospitality to packaging, menus and brand production. | Ground the illustrative founder profile in specific decisions made on set. Describe collaboration and deliverables without invented credits. |
| [Shamanth Patil Photography](https://shamanthpatil.photography/) | A wide architectural view preserves perspective and foreground depth. A single portfolio action sits within the image. | In interiors projects, lead with an uncropped room view and follow with material details. Keep text clear of the important sightline. |

## Art direction for the build

- Give the home page one lead subject and a short route into that project. Alternate wide and intimate compositions over the scroll. Mobile needs its own crop and a real break or space between headline words.
- Use an ink and paper palette for sustained reading. Reserve the orange accent for the few places where the visitor acts. Use small editorial captions to distinguish the project, medium or type of shoot.
- Open the project with the intended use of the pictures. Sequence environment, subject and detail. Preserve aspects in the viewer and allow both touch and keyboard browsing.
- Lead each service with concrete outputs. An e-commerce listing, an event recap and a private portrait need different preparation and delivery decisions.
- Let visitors compare a defined package with the cost of appropriate additions. Custom commercial work needs its own brief with usage, production and deadline. A local review, copy or download can be useful before either party contacts a real studio.
- Review the first mobile viewport independently. The photo, identity, work and enquiry actions should still be intelligible before the visitor opens a menu.
