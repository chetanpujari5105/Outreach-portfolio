# Frame & Field photography inventory

## Delivery

The library contains **73 photographs**, covering 16 editorial projects and the fictional Pune studio. Each photograph has a full-size WebP and a 640-pixel-wide derivative. All images load from the site’s own origin. The 146 WebPs occupy 11.5 MiB on disk. The largest individual file is commercial-b4.webp at 286 KiB.

[Image manifest](../../studio/brands/frame-field/photos.mjs) · [Optimized files](../../studio/public/assets/frame-field/)

The original implementation supplied four dedicated Frame & Field photographs, plus images borrowed from the salon, retail and event demos. This edition retains three dedicated original frames (portrait, perfume and stage). The separate bespoke portfolio series replace the borrowed filler. The full collection contains 70 newly generated photographs from the built-in ChatGPT image tool. People, premises and assignments in this concept are illustrative.

## Art direction and verification

The home cover uses a 3:2 composition in scarlet and dusty rose. Place the sitter toward the right edge of the crop on narrow screens. Subjects extend beyond the hero into quiet portraits, hard-lit still lifes, architectural studies and documentary sequences. Review used contact sheets for the 16 sequences and studio material, followed by dimension and decode checks on the originals and their responsive exports. Each project opens with an establishing photograph and changes scale over the following images.

The following series descriptions and shot directions form the generation brief and source index. Each new frame was requested as one standalone photograph using its series description and the shot directions below. The common instructions specified believable lens and material detail, restrained film grain and no text, logos, overlays or watermarks. The photos are illustrations for a fictional studio and do not document real commissions.
