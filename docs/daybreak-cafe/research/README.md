# Daybreak Café: visual research (2026-11-24)

First-screen desktop captures (1920×1080), plus Dishoom on a phone (360×800). Contact sheets are in this folder.

Limits: I compared the first screen of each site only, not full pages or flows. Daybreak was judged on full pages, so the comparison is about first impressions.
Flour + Water resolved to a bread-making site of that name, not the San Francisco restaurant; it counts for typography only. Araku may have been captured mid-load.
That leaves 10 café, bakery, coffee and restaurant sites, with Araku the weakest evidence.
% Arabica (arabica.com) refused automated capture twice (HTTP 418), so it is not included.

| Site | What I saw | What Daybreak takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| Subko (subko.coffee) | Full-bleed landscape photo, one centred all-caps statement, very quiet nav that includes Locations and Contact | Restraint: one strong statement per screen, not one per section |
| Tartine (tartinebakery.com) | Soft bakery photo, monospaced intro, location counts sitting inside the hero | Practical facts belong in the first screen, not the footer |
| Onyx Coffee Lab | Deep brand colour, a script mark used as a graphic, almost no copy | One ownable brand device, used with confidence |
| Araku (possibly mid-load) | Very large wordmark on black | Scale contrast, as a weak signal only |
| Blue Tokai | Retail banners, round category icons, chat widget | The counter-example: busy, promotional, generic. Avoid |
| The Bombay Canteen | Top bar "Open all days 12 pm to 1:30 am. Book a table.", full-bleed interior photo, illustrated logo | Hours and booking stated before anything else; the room sells the visit |
| Kurasu (Kyoto) | Product photo with a small card overlay | Small, precise info cards over photography |
| Flour + Water (bread site) | Bold display type over a textural bread photo | Texture and typography can carry a bakery |
| Dishoom (phone) | "Book" stays in the header beside the logo; cookie banner covers half the screen | Booking always one tap away (Daybreak's phone dock); nothing covering the first screen |
| SEY | Origin photography (coffee cherries), tiny type, lots of air | Origin storytelling through images, not paragraphs |
| Prufrock | One latte-art photo, centred condensed headline | One cup photographed well is enough |

## Daybreak before this pass
- The hero is strong (it matches the best of the above), but the next two phone screens were a text-heavy welcome story, so food appeared only on screen three.
- 15 home sections, each with the same "kicker + serif heading + italic phrase" rhythm, which reads as a template.
- Opening hours and booking were in the hero foot only as small text.

## Changes made from this research
1. Food straight after the hero; the welcome story is removed from home (it lives on Our Story).
2. A practical "today" strip in the hero (hours, directions, booking), in the spirit of The Bombay Canteen's top bar.
3. The sunrise mark becomes Daybreak's recurring graphic device (section kickers), instead of a generic rule.
4. Home cut from 15 to 13 sections, the day scroller shortened, and the duplicate closing banner removed.
